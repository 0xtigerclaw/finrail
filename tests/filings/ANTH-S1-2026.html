Adjusted Operating Income (Loss) and Adjusted Operating Margin
We define Adjusted Operating Income (Loss) as operating loss excluding (i) stock-based compensation expense,
(ii) non-cash charitable contributions, and (iii) the Bartz settlement. We define Adjusted Operating Margin as
Adjusted Operating Income (Loss) divided by revenue.
